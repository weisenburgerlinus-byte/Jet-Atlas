Su-57.

Multirole fighter 5th generation

The Su-57 is a multirole fighter developed by the Sukhoi design bureau. UAC describes it as an aircraft designed for missions against air, ground and sea targets.

Designed for multiple roles

Development

First flight in 2010

The aircraft's first flight took place on January 29, 2010, in Komsomolsk-on-Amur. It was developed by the Sukhoi designs bureau.
