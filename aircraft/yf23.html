YF-23 Black Widow II.

Advanced tactical fighter demonstrator

Developed by a Northrop and McDonnell Douglas team, the YF-23 the competed in the U.S. Air Force's Advanced Tactical Fighter program. Two prototypes took part in the evaluation.

Design & outcome

A prototype, not a production fighter

The YF-23 was built to demonstrate a design for the Air Force's Advanced Tactical Fighter competition. In 1991, the Air Force selected the competing YF-22 design for the further development.

The two YF-23 prototypes were later transferred to museums.
