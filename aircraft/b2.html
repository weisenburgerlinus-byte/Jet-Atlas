B-2 Spirit.

Long-range Strategic bomber

The B-2 Spirit is a two-pilot heavy bomber developed by a Northrop Grumman-led contractor team. Its flying-wing design combines long range with low-observable features.

Weapons & payload

What can the B-2 carry?

The B-2 carries different kinds of munition in two internal bays. Its payload depends on the mission and weapon configuration.

Payload capacity U.S. Air Force

60,000 pounds

The Air Force fact sheet lists a 60,000-pound payload. A separate test demonstrated the release of 80 inert 500-pound JDAMs.

  • JDAM Precision-guided conventional bomb
  • GBU-57 MOP Guided earth-penetrating munition

These are separate public examples, not one combined loadout. The 80-JDAM figure describes an inert test configuration, not a standard operational load.

01 JDAM Guided conventional bomb

Role: A precision-guided air-to-surface weapon used with compatible bomb bodies.

How it works:A guidance tail kit combines control surfaces with GPS-aided inertial navigation. After release, the system guides the bomb toward its programmed coordinates.

At a glance

Built for long-range missions

The U.S. Air Force describes the B-2 as a multi-role heavy bomber with intercontinental range. It reached initial operating capability in 1997.

Design & role

A flying-wing design

The B-2's flying-wing shape, composite materials and other low-observable features help reduce its signatures. Some details of the aircraft's low-observable design remain classified.

Northrop Grumman was the prime contractor, working with a wider team that included Boeing, Hughes, General Electric and Vought.
