B-2 Spirit.

Long-range Strategic bomber

The B-2 Spirit is a two-pilot heavy bomber developed by a Northrop Grumman-led contractor team. Its flying-wing design combines long range with low-observable features.

At a glance

Built for long-range missions

The U.S. Air Force describes the B-2 as a multi-role heavy bomber with intercontinental range. It reached initial operating capability in 1997.

Design & role

A flying-wing design

The B-2's flying-wing shape, composite materials and other low-observable features help reduce its signatures. Some details of the aircraft's low-observable design remain classified.

Northrop Grumman was the prime contractor, working with a wider team that included Boeing, Hughes, General Electric and Vought.
